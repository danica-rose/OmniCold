Temperature Breach Detection

When the reading goes out of range, the bond is slashed instantly (~5 seconds)

❄️ Too Cold ✅ Safe Zone (2°C – 8°C) 🔥 Too Hot
⚠️ BREACH DETECTED — Bond slashed to Shipper

Three Roles

Each participant has a specific function in the lifecycle

📦
Shipper
Creates shipments, sets thresholds, confirms delivery. Receives slashed bonds on breach.
🚛
Provider
Deposits USDC bond as service guarantee. Bond released on successful delivery.
📡
Oracle
Reports IoT temperature readings on-chain. Triggers automatic breach detection.
